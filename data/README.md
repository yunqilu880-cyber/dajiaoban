# Public example records / 公开样本来源

Contains information from **Gait in Parkinson’s Disease v1.0.0**, contributed by Jeffrey Hausdorff and hosted by PhysioNet, made available under the **Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0**.

- Dataset: https://physionet.org/content/gaitpdb/1.0.0/
- License: https://physionet.org/content/gaitpdb/view-license/1.0.0/
- GaCo01_01.txt: https://physionet.org/files/gaitpdb/1.0.0/GaCo01_01.txt
- GaPt03_01.txt: https://physionet.org/files/gaitpdb/1.0.0/GaPt03_01.txt
- Format and arbitrary-scale coordinates: https://physionet.org/content/gaitpdb/1.0.0/format.txt

The TXT samples and format.txt are unmodified byte-for-byte copies. SHA-256 values are recorded in records.json. The included license text is a plain-text snapshot of the official license page, retaining the license terms.

本网站选择两份去标识化公开记录用于教学回放，不代表自行招募或完成166人的研究。原始分组属于数据集背景，不构成网站对使用者的疾病判断。受力单位为牛顿，点位坐标是任意缩放的相对坐标，不代表毫米级位置。

Our original browser-side analysis is version ZYK-GAIT-0.3. It includes per-point statistics, same-record segment comparison, traceable quality checks, event-derived engineering cycles with 101-point phase overlays, and relative-coordinate CoP proxies. Applied settings and full derived arrays are included in JSON reports; local-file geometry requires confirmation. Means, forces and integrals are observations; contact events are heuristic candidates, not validated gait labels. Full source references and current citation requirements are available on the dataset page.
